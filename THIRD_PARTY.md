# Third-party fonts

The Wayline theme bundles LXGW WenKai for rounded display headings under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Body text and monospace metadata use the reader's
system fonts to reduce initial network requests.

| Font | Use | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LXGW WenKai | rounded Chinese display headings | [LXGW WenKai](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai) | OFL-1.1 |

The complete LXGW WenKai license is stored at
`static/fonts/licenses/OFL-LXGWWenKai.txt`.
