# Third-party fonts

The Wayline theme bundles the following fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The complete license texts are stored in `static/fonts/licenses/`.

| Font | Use | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Space Grotesk | display, Latin text, numbers | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk) / [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/fonts/space-grotesk) | OFL-1.1 |
| Noto Sans SC | Chinese text | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+SC) / [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/fonts/noto-sans-sc) | OFL-1.1 |
| LXGW WenKai | rounded Chinese display headings | [LXGW WenKai](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai) | OFL-1.1 |
| JetBrains Mono | code, labels, metadata | [JetBrains](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) / [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/fonts/jetbrains-mono) | OFL-1.1 |

The Noto CJK fonts are subset to the characters used by this site to keep the payload small.
Characters outside the subset fall back to the reader's installed Chinese system font.
